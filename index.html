What Cleared does

One paper sheet, turned into a plan that keeps up with you.

Photograph the instructions your surgeon gave you, or tap a few in yourself. Cleared reads them, lays out every step against your surgery date, and does the remembering so you do not have to.

Reads your sheet

Snap a photo of your prep instructions. Cleared pulls out each medication to hold, each fasting cutoff, and each task, then shows you exactly what it found so you can confirm every line.

Counts down for you

Every instruction becomes a dated reminder anchored to your surgery. Separate clocks count down your last solid food and last clear liquid, each with a plain, honest definition.

Explains and answers

Each hold comes with a short, guideline-sourced reason, framed as education about the instruction you already have. A bounded assistant answers questions grounded in your own sheet.

Pre-Surgery Instructions
Orthopedics Clinic · Knee arthroscopy

Please read all of these instructions carefully and call the pre-admission clinic if anything is unclear before the day of your operation.

Medications. Stop taking ibuprofen, naproxen and other anti-inflammatory pain relievers 7 days before your surgery. Continue your usual blood pressure tablets with a small sip of water on the morning of the operation unless told otherwise. If you take a blood thinner or a diabetes medicine, contact your surgeon for individual advice.

Eating and drinking. Do not eat any solid food after midnight the night before surgery. You may keep drinking clear liquids such as water, apple juice or black tea until 2 hours before your arrival time. No milk, gum or sweets after that point.

Before you arrive. Shower with the chlorhexidine wash the night before and again on the morning of surgery. Remove jewellery, nail polish and make-up, and arrange for a responsible adult to drive you home.

Built to HIPAA-grade privacy standards

Your medical information never leaves your phone.

The safest data is data that was never sent anywhere. Cleared is built on-device by default, so there is no central database of patients to breach, and nothing to trust a stranger with.

On-device by default

Capture, reading, your plan, and your reminders all run on your own phone. Your instruction sheet is not uploaded to a server.

Encrypted at rest

Your plan is stored in the phone's secure, encrypted storage, protected by the same hardware that guards the rest of your device.

Locked behind you

Face ID, Touch ID, or your Android biometric opens the app, so a lost or borrowed phone never exposes your medication list.

Quiet notifications

Push, email, and text reminders stay generic. They tell you to open the app, never which drug to stop, so nothing leaks to a lock screen.

Because Cleared keeps your data on your device, a purely personal use of the app sits outside the reach of most central-server risks. The evidence

The instructions are right. The last mile is where it breaks.

Cleared is built on a simple, well-documented finding: surgeries are cancelled every day for reasons that have nothing to do with the surgery itself. A step at home was forgotten or misunderstood. Below are the peer-reviewed studies and analyses behind the product, and what each one found.

The studies behind Cleared

Each link opens the source on PubMed or PubMed Central. Findings are summarized in plain language; the originals hold the full method and data.

01
Adherence

Preoperative medication adherence in a pre-admission clinic

Assessing Patient Understanding and Adherence to Preoperative Medication Advice · PMC12524066

Only 58.3% of patients adhered to their preoperative medication advice. Among those who did not, the leading reasons were forgetting it (35.4%) and misunderstanding it (33.8%). Adherence worsened as more medications were involved and as the gap to surgery grew. Two cases were cancelled outright for delayed medication cessation.

View source
02
Cancellations

A mobile app for major ambulatory surgery preparation (Listeo+)

Effect of a Mobile App on Preoperative Patient Preparation, RCT protocol · PMC6352007

Estimated that roughly 25% of cancellations in major ambulatory surgery come from poor compliance with preoperative recommendations, and are therefore avoidable. This is the exact slice of the problem Cleared is built to shrink.

View source
03
Cancellations

Elective case cancellation in an academic center

Dynamics of Elective Case Cancellation for Inpatient and Outpatient · PubMed 24286019

Day-of-surgery cancellation rate of 6.6%. The single most common cause in the general operating room was inadequate preoperative preparation, ahead of medical changes and scheduling, with about 59% of cancellations judged preventable.

View source
04
Cancellations

Rates and reasons of same-day cancellations, multicentre

Assessing the Rates and Reasons of Elective Surgical Cancellations · PMC8724145

A multicentre audit found an overall cancellation rate near 9.7%, with 74% of those cancellations judged avoidable, alongside a national estimate of the resulting financial loss.

View source
05
Cancellations

Causes of cancellation at a tertiary teaching hospital

Causes of Cancellations on the Day of Surgery · PMC4944432

Reported a 4.4% cancellation rate with 71.6% potentially avoidable, and concluded that a rate under 2% is attainable with better systems and preparation.

View source
06
Context

Reducing day-of-surgery cancellations, review

Evidence-based Approaches Toward Reducing Cancellations on the Day of Surgery · PMC4268531

Reviews reported cancellation rates ranging from about 1% up to a quarter of elective outpatient cases and up to two-thirds of inpatient cases, depending on setting and case mix.

View source
07
Cost

The cost of operating room time

Childers & Maggard-Gibbons, Understanding Costs of Care in the Operating Room · JAMA Surg 2018

Analysis of California hospital financial data placed the cost of one minute of operating room time at roughly $36 to $37, a figure that makes even a handful of prevented cancellations financially meaningful.

View source
08
Cost

Root causes and cost of surgery cancellations

Incidence and Root Causes of Surgery Cancellations at an Academic Medical Center · PMC10481593

Cites an average cancelled operation cost in the range of roughly $5,000 to $8,000, reinforcing the economic case for closing the preparation gap.

Fasting

Fasting non-adherence and its causes

Point-of-care Gastric Ultrasound in Patients with Fasting Non-adherence · PMC11247740

Traces fasting violations, a leading cause of same-day cancellation, to misunderstanding, language barriers, and scheduling changes that made the original instruction wrong.

Common questions

Quick answers

The things people ask most before they download. If yours is not here, the inbox above is happy to help.

Is Cleared really free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no account required and no medical data leaving your phone.

Do you see my medications?

No. Your plan is built and stored on your device. There is no central database of patient data for anyone to access.

Does my hospital need to sign up?

No. Cleared works with only the paper instruction sheet you already have. Photograph it, or tap the steps in yourself.

What if my surgery date changes?

Change one field. Every reminder and both fasting clocks recompute instantly, so your whole plan stays correct.

Do I need to make an account?

No. An account is entirely optional. Open the app, set your surgery date, and start building your plan right away, or create an account if you prefer.

What if I have no printed sheet?

You can add restrictions by hand with quick taps, or start from a procedure template and edit it. No photo needed.

Can my caregiver get reminders?

Yes. Add a spouse or adult child so they receive the same reminders and can help you stay on track.

Is this medical advice?

No. Cleared organizes and reminds you of the instructions your care team already gave you. It never creates new medical instructions.
